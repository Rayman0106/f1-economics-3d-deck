## 答问备用

以下不计入 8 分钟。

### 维修一定挤掉研发吗

Only if the extra repairs count towards covered costs and the spending limit is binding. Spare parts, accounting treatment and unused headroom can change the effect.

### 200 万美元是真实账单吗

No. The ten, two and eight million figures are a hypothetical allocation example. The presentation does not establish the actual repair bill for the Baku incident.

### 成本上限会不会抑制创新

It can reduce the amount of development funded by the wealthiest teams. That is a trade-off. My argument depends on choosing a level that still allows meaningful engineering competition.

### 怎么证明竞争已经变得更公平

This presentation explains a mechanism and the policy's objectives. To establish its effect, we would need evidence about covered spending and performance, while allowing for other changes in the sport.

### 为什么凯迪拉克还会落后

A spending limit does not instantly provide experience, effective working routines or technical capability. A new team still has to develop those advantages.
